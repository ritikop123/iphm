How it works

Three steps, start to finish. Usually takes under ten minutes.

  1. 01

    Pick a provider

    Every listing shows the country, machine type, top PPS, NIC speed, and whether spoofing still works. The name and site stay hidden until you pay.

  2. 02

    Pay in crypto (USD pricing)

    All listings are priced in US Dollars ($45, $75, $110, $60). Choose Litecoin (LTC), Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, USDT, or DOGE. Send the exact amount, paste your TXID, and we track the chain.

  3. 03

    Get the name and link

    Once your payment has 2 confirmations, it unlocks on its own. The provider's name and website show up in your dashboard for good.
